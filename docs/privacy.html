Orbit — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 August 2026

Orbit is a casual arcade game published by Foundry7 (contact below). This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Data we collect

  • Nickname — the display name you choose at first launch, shown on the public leaderboard.
  • Device identifier — a random identifier tied to your device, used to key your single leaderboard account. It is not your name, phone number, or Apple ID.
  • Game scores — your best scores per week, month, and all time, stored with your nickname and avatar choice.

This data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore). We do not collect your email, contacts, location, or any other personal information.

Advertising

Orbit shows ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, subject to your App Tracking Transparency choice) and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. If you decline tracking, ads are still shown but are not personalized. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in AdMob.

Data deletion

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete Account. This removes your nickname and leaderboard scores from our servers. You can also email us to request deletion.

Children

Orbit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
